Your homelab at a glance

A native dashboard for the services you host yourself — iPhone, iPad and Mac. It tells you which ones are running, which ones are dragging, and which one just went down.

Join the TestFlight beta Source code

What it does

  • The real metrics of over sixty services, read through their own API: Jellyfin movies and series, Immich photos, AdGuard blocked queries, free space on the NAS, downloads in flight.
  • Outage detection after three attempts: a notification, a Live Activity, and a status wall that keeps thirty days of history.
  • Running in three minutes: a Bonjour scan of your network, an import of a services.yaml in gethomepage.dev format, or an address typed by hand.
  • Widgets fed from live state, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen alike.

What it doesn't do

No account, no server in the middle, no telemetry. The app queries your machines directly, and your API keys stay in your device's Keychain. See the privacy policy.

Compatibility

iOS and iPadOS 26, macOS 26. English, French, Spanish, Simplified Chinese and Arabic.
