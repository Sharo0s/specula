Status page

The availability wall comes to iPhone and iPad, and gets rebuilt on Mac: one
row per service, one square per day, and any incident one tap away.

Three states instead of two — online, slow, offline. A service that starts
dragging shows up before it goes down.

Services in trouble move to the top, every column carries its date, and the
window can be set to 7, 14 or 30 days.
