Specula watches the services you host yourself and tells you at a glance which
ones are running, which are struggling and which one just went down.

No account, no middleman server, no telemetry: the app queries your machines
directly on your network. Your API keys stay on your device.

Every service shows its real metrics, read through its own API: Immich's photo
count, AdGuard's blocked queries, your NAS's free space, Proxmox's containers.
Service types are recognised automatically.

iPhone, iPad and Mac, with widgets on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, a
Live Activity during an outage and access from the Mac menu bar.

TESTING WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING

Demo mode, offered right in the walkthrough, fills the app with simulated data.
You can go through everything without giving a single address.

TESTING AGAINST YOUR OWN SERVICES

Three ways in: scan the network, import a services.yaml from Homepage, or add
an address by hand. The first four services are free.

PURCHASES ARE FREE IN TESTFLIGHT

Extra slots and the "unlimited services" option go through the sandbox: nothing
is charged to you. Do try them, it helps.

REPORTING A PROBLEM

Take a screenshot, then "Send Feedback" from TestFlight, or open an issue at
github.com/Sharo0s/specula. Name the service and its type: most mismatches come
from an API answering differently than expected.
